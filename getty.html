Flemish Still Life, c.1689 — Unknown Master
c. 1689

Painted in Antwerp

A Flemish master completes this still life in his Antwerp atelier. The painting depicts a table laden with precious objects — a rummer of wine, exotic shells, and a gilded nautilus cup — symbols of mercantile wealth in the Dutch Golden Age.
